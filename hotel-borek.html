Hotel Borek

A developing evidence-led archive of the hotel as part of the industrial, railway and commercial landscape around Borek and Zbiroh station.

Why Hotel Borek matters

Hotel Borek is being researched as a historical subject in its own right and as part of the wider Borek industrial and railway system. The archive is especially interested in ownership, commercial travel, railway functions, company visitors and the relationship between the hotel, factory and station.

Current evidence status

Industrial era
Research in progress

Ownership by the industrial enterprise

Factory ownership of Hotel Borek is a central proposition in the research. Precise dates and legal form are being checked against primary ownership, company and cadastral records.

Before the large station complex
Research in progress

Hotel and railway functions

The project is investigating evidence that railway passenger or administrative functions were associated with Hotel Borek before the later purpose-built station complex.

Central European commercial period
Research in progress

Travelling commercial network

The archive is examining the hotel as accommodation and a meeting point for people travelling in connection with the industrial enterprise across Central Europe.
