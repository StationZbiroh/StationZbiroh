Hotel Borek

A developing evidence-led archive of the hotel as part of the industrial, railway and commercial landscape around Borek and Zbiroh station.

Why Hotel Borek matters

Hotel Borek is being researched as a historical subject in its own right, while also being examined as part of the wider Borek industrial and railway system. The archive is especially interested in ownership, commercial travel, railway functions, company visitors and the relationship between the hotel, factory and station.

The public page will grow only as documentary evidence is added.

Current evidence status

Industrial era
Research in progress

Ownership by the industrial enterprise

Factory ownership of Hotel Borek is a central proposition in the current research. The archive is seeking the primary ownership record, company documentation or cadastral evidence that establishes the precise dates and legal form of that ownership before presenting it as a fully documented timeline entry.

Before the large station complex
Research in progress

Hotel and railway functions

The project is investigating evidence that railway passenger or administrative functions were associated with Hotel Borek before the later purpose-built station complex. The exact offices, dates and institutional arrangements remain to be documented from railway or archival sources.

Central European commercial period
Research in progress

Travelling commercial network

The archive is examining the hotel as a possible accommodation and meeting point for people travelling in connection with the industrial enterprise across Central Europe. Guest registers, advertisements, company correspondence, trade directories and railway records are priority sources.

Research branches

Ownership

Establish who owned the hotel, when ownership changed, and whether changes tracked the ownership history of the Borek enterprise.

Hotel and factory

Document whether the hotel accommodated representatives, customers, suppliers, engineers or other visitors connected with industrial activity.

Hotel and railway

Reconstruct the relationship between the hotel, the railway stop, freight and passenger traffic, and the later Zbiroh station complex.

People

Identify documented owners, managers, employees and guests and connect them to the wider historical network only where evidence supports the relationship.

Images and maps

Collect postcards, photographs, cadastral maps, railway plans and aerial imagery showing the hotel and its surroundings over time.

Later history

Establish later uses, alterations, ownership changes, survival or demolition through dated documentary evidence.

Proč je Hotel Borek důležitý

Hotel Borek je zkoumán jako samostatné historické téma a současně jako součást širšího průmyslového a železničního systému Borku. Výzkum se zaměřuje zejména na vlastnictví, obchodní cestování, železniční funkce a vztah mezi hotelem, továrnou a nádražím.

Veřejná stránka se bude rozšiřovat pouze s přibývajícími doloženými prameny.

Warum Hotel Borek wichtig ist

Hotel Borek wird als eigenständiges historisches Thema und zugleich als Teil des größeren Industrie- und Eisenbahnsystems von Borek untersucht. Im Mittelpunkt stehen Eigentum, Geschäftsreisen, Eisenbahnfunktionen und die Beziehung zwischen Hotel, Fabrik und Bahnhof.
