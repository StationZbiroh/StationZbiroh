Research in progress

Post-Strousberg ownership

Reconstruct the nineteenth-century ownership sequence directly from cadastral and archival records, including crossed-out or amended owner names.

Industry and the station

Test whether industrial freight, offices, accommodation, land or capital influenced the size and functions of the early-twentieth-century station complex.

Depression-era recalibration

Establish what changed in ownership, production, staffing, corporate form and branding during the 1930s.

Occupation and wartime use

Document factory production, railway use, military activity and any relationship to Zbiroh Castle using attributable wartime sources.

Nationalisation and Zbirovia

Test whether machinery, people, patents, designs, products or other assets passed between Borek industry and Zbirovia after 1945.

Approximate c.1952 disappearance

The proposed early-1950s dismantling/demolition chronology remains unverified and will not be stated as fact until supported by dated records or imagery.

Editorial note. This first public version is intentionally incomplete. A missing date is preferable to an invented date; a labelled hypothesis is preferable to a confident but unsupported story.
