Stanice Zbiroh · Historical Research Archive

Timeline of a railway, an industrial landscape, and the people connected to it

A public, evidence-led research project. Claims are separated from hypotheses, sources remain visible, and unresolved questions remain unresolved.

Research standard

The archive follows a simple rule: the strength of the language must not exceed the strength of the evidence.

Evidence → context → interpretation → conclusion.
