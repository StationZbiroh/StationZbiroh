# Primary Source Documents

This directory contains digitised and compiled primary-source documents used by the Stanice Zbiroh Historical Research Archive.

## Intended contents

Historical newspaper extracts, archival scans, contemporary publications, maps, catalogues and other source material cited by the archive.

The source, date and provenance of individual documents should be recorded on the archive page that links to them.
