Source catalogue

A consolidated guide to the archive’s principal documents and source groups. It records what each item is, where it came from, what it can establish and what cataloguing work remains.

Cataloguing note: This is a developing finding aid. “Held in the archive” means that a digital image, scan or research copy is present in this repository or linked collection; it does not necessarily mean that the project owns the physical original or its reproduction rights.

People and remembrance

Victim recordIndustrial identity questions remain

Emil Brandeis victim record

The Holocaust.cz database identifies Emil Brandeis, born in Kařez in 1868, and records deportation to Terezín and subsequently Treblinka. The relationship between this documented person and particular industrial records is evaluated separately.

Records still sought

  • Original construction, design and railway-administration files for Zbiroh station.
  • Company-register and liquidation records for the Brandeis–Eisenschimmel enterprise.
  • Original deeds, land-book entries and auction records clarifying the post-1875 property chains.
  • Page-level catalogue metadata: exact title, date, printer, pagination and repository reference.
  • Primary British judicial or administrative evidence for the reported 1847 Strousberg conviction.
  • Patent-register evidence clarifying the Aurich silo system and legal ownership.
