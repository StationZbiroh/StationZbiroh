# Stanice Zbiroh — Historical Research Archive

An open research archive documenting the history of Zbiroh railway station and the surrounding area, including Borek, local industry, transport, landscape, and twentieth-century history.

## Central research question

Why was Zbiroh station, built around 1910, constructed on such a substantial scale?

One line of research is the relationship between the station and the thriving local industry associated with the Brandeis–Eisenschimmel joint-stock company and its industrial network. This repository will collect evidence for, against, and around that hypothesis rather than treating it as a settled conclusion.

## Research areas

- Zbiroh railway station and its development
- Borek and local industrial history
- Železárna Borek and the Brandeis–Eisenschimmel company
- Company, commercial-register and liquidation records
- Historical maps, cadastral material and aerial photography
- Railway and transport infrastructure
- Local landscape and natural heritage, including significant trees
- Jewish history and the Holocaust
- Second World War history in the surrounding area
- Archival correspondence and research enquiries
- Sources, photographs, documents and unresolved research questions

## Research method

The archive aims to distinguish clearly between:

1. **Primary evidence** — archival documents, maps, photographs, registers and contemporary records.
2. **Secondary sources** — published historical research and later accounts.
3. **Working hypotheses** — interpretations that remain to be tested against evidence.
4. **Research correspondence** — a record of archives, institutions and researchers contacted and the results of those enquiries.

Contradictory evidence will be retained rather than silently reconciled. Where a claim is uncertain, the archive will say so.

## Languages

The public archive is intended to develop in three languages:

- Czech
- English
- German

This is intended both to make the material accessible locally and internationally and to improve discoverability of the historical record.

## Status

This repository is at the beginning of a long-term archival research project. Material will be added progressively and source citations improved as documents are catalogued.
