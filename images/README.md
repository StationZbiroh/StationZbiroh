# Image upload area

Upload photographs, scans, postcards, maps and other visual material relevant to the Stanice Zbiroh Historical Research Archive into this `images/` folder.

You do not need to decide where each image belongs before uploading it. The archive can later classify and place images under relevant subjects such as:

- Stanice Zbiroh
- Borek industrial complex
- Hotel Borek
- Bethel Henry Strousberg
- Brandeis–Eisenschimmel
- surviving buildings and present-day site photographs
- historical postcards and photographs
- maps, plans and aerial imagery
- people and archival documents

Where possible, keep the original file and avoid destructive editing. Descriptive filenames are useful but not required at upload stage; files can be renamed and catalogued later.

Suggested eventual naming pattern: `subject-date-description-source.jpg`.

For every image used publicly, the archive should record whatever is known about date, source/provenance, photographer or rights holder, location, and evidence status.
