Hotel Borek · Textual evidence

Read What Was Written on the Postcard

A transcription and interpretation of the printed wording preserved on the historical Hotel Borek postcard.

Printed text

HOTEL BOREK
Zbiroh - Nádraží.
Velký sál měří 405 m².

English translation

HOTEL BOREK
Zbiroh — Station.
The Great Hall measures 405 m².

What the wording tells us

The wording is unusually economical. It identifies the building, ties it directly to Zbiroh station, and then chooses one specific fact to advertise: the size of the Great Hall. That makes the 405 m² figure part of the postcard’s original promotional message rather than a later measurement added by researchers.

The use of “Velký sál” is also important. It translates directly as “Great Hall” or “Large Hall”, showing that the space was understood and presented as a principal feature of the hotel.

Photographic attribution

The reverse also carries the printed attribution “Fotografický atelier M. Bickert, Rokycany”, identifying the photographic studio associated with the postcard.

A separate line or number visible with the studio imprint may help with dating or catalogue identification, but it should not be treated as a confirmed date until independently verified.

Handwritten message and postal markings

The surviving postcard also contains handwriting, address information, stamp and postmark evidence. A full diplomatic transcription of that handwritten material should be published separately once every character can be read with confidence from the original source image.

This page deliberately separates clearly legible printed text from handwriting that still requires source-level transcription.
