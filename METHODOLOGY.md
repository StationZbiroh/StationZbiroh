# Research Methodology

## Purpose

The Stanice Zbiroh Historical Research Archive documents the station, Borek industrial landscape, neighbouring communities, people and institutions through evidence-led historical research.

The methodological benchmark is rigorous documentary history: evidence first, context second, interpretation third, conclusion last. The archive does not collect documents merely to confirm an existing story.

## Evidence status

Every substantial historical claim should, where practical, be assigned one of these states:

- **Documented** — directly supported by identifiable primary evidence.
- **Strongly supported** — supported by multiple reliable sources, though primary documentation may be incomplete.
- **Plausible hypothesis** — consistent with available evidence but not demonstrated.
- **Disputed** — credible sources or evidence conflict.
- **Unverified** — reported or suspected but presently lacking adequate evidence.

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary archival records, contemporary maps, cadastral records, photographs, company registers, railway records and contemporary publications.
2. Scholarly and institutional secondary research.
3. Local histories and later recollections, identified as such.
4. Working research notes and hypotheses.

A lower category is not automatically false. Its evidentiary status must simply remain visible.

## Provenance and links

Records should preserve, where available: archive/institution; collection or fonds; reference/signature; document title; creator; date; language; page/image number; stable external URL; access date; and a local archival record ID.

External links should point to the originating archive, catalogue, authority record, museum, database or scholarly resource whenever possible. Links are evidence trails, not substitutes for citations.

## Contradictions and corrections

Contradictory evidence is retained and described. Uncertainty is not silently resolved. If new evidence changes a conclusion, the interpretation should be revised while the repository history preserves the earlier state.

## Working rule

**Evidence → context → interpretation → conclusion.**

The strength of the language used on the public site must not exceed the strength of the evidence.
