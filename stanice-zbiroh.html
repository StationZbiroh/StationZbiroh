How this page works

This page separates documented evidence from interpretation. Historical photographs, postcards, plans, maps and archival records will be arranged chronologically. Where a date, event or explanation has not yet been established, it will remain an open research question.

Chronology

1911–1912

The new Zbiroh station

The present station building was constructed during the rebuilding and doubling of the Prague–Plzeň railway. Surviving heritage documentation identifies the project with the State Railways Directorate in Prague and builder Vendelín Hron. Further primary documentation will be added as it is located.

Early 20th century · exact postcard date under research

Station and Hotel Borek before the motorway

The postcard above preserves the physical relationship between the new station, Hotel Borek and the surrounding landscape. The foreground is low-lying and wet, while the slopes behind the buildings are substantially more open and sparsely wooded than today. The reason for the difference in tree cover has not yet been established.

October 1992–26 October 1995

The D5 transforms the landscape

Construction of the D5 motorway section Bavoryně–Mýto began in October 1992. The section was divided into Bavoryně–Kařez and Kařez–Mýto; both were opened to traffic on 26 October 1995. The motorway fundamentally altered the earlier landscape recorded in the postcard. The archive is continuing to investigate the precise relationship between the historical wet ground, former access routes and the later motorway alignment.

Historical station photographs and postcards are being assembled in a dedicated source-image collection. Images will be presented with dates and provenance where known, and uncertain dating will be identified as such.

Evidence that corrects or challenges the chronology is equally welcome.
