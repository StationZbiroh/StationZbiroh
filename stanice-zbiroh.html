Stanice Zbiroh

A chronological, evidence-led history of the former Zbiroh railway station: why it was built, how the building and surrounding landscape changed, what happened here, and what remains unresolved.

← Historical Research Archive

How this page works

This page separates documented evidence from interpretation. Historical photographs, postcards, plans, maps and archival records will be arranged chronologically. Where a date, event or explanation has not yet been established, it will remain an open research question.

Chronology

1911–1912

The new Zbiroh station

The present station building was constructed during the rebuilding and doubling of the Prague–Plzeň railway. Surviving heritage documentation identifies the project with the State Railways Directorate in Prague and builder Vendelín Hron. Further primary documentation will be added as it is located.

Early station landscape

Station and Hotel Borek

Period postcards show the new station together with Hotel Borek and preserve the surrounding landscape before later road construction and development. These images are important evidence for the physical relationship between the station, hotel, access routes and low-lying land nearby.

20th century

A changing railway and landscape

This section will document alterations to the station, railway infrastructure and surrounding land in chronological order as photographs and archival evidence are assembled.

Research in progress

Later use and survival

The later history of the building, changes in railway use, alterations and preservation will be developed from dated documentary evidence rather than retrospective assumptions.

Source-image archive

Historical station photographs and postcards are being assembled in a dedicated source-image collection. Images will be presented with dates and provenance where known, and uncertain dating will be identified as such.

Contribute evidence

If you have historical photographs, postcards, plans, railway documents, personal recollections or other material relating to Zbiroh station, Hotel Borek or the surrounding railway landscape, we would be interested in hearing from you. Evidence that corrects or challenges the chronology is equally welcome.
