Page-by-page research register

An editorial map of what each public page is for, what kind of evidence it contains, and what would most improve its historical reliability.

This register assesses documentation, not historical importance. A page marked “working interpretation” may contain valuable evidence; the label indicates the level at which its present conclusions should be read.

Editorial rule for future pages

Every new page should state its purpose, identify its evidential character, link to the underlying source, separate observation from interpretation, list unresolved questions and appear in both the source catalogue and this register. The register should be updated whenever a conclusion materially changes.
