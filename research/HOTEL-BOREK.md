# Hotel Borek — Research Branch

## Purpose

Hotel Borek is treated as a distinct research subject within the Stanice Zbiroh Historical Research Archive. Its history intersects with the Borek industrial complex, railway development, commercial travel and the wider social history of the area, but it should not be reduced to a footnote in the factory history.

## Evidence standard

This page is presently a **research framework**. Statements about ownership, guests, commercial functions, railway use and dates must be supported by attributable documentary evidence before being promoted to documented fact on the public site.

## Principal research questions

### Ownership and establishment

- When was Hotel Borek constructed or established?
- What historical names and spelling variants did it use?
- What documentary evidence establishes ownership by the Borek industrial enterprise, and during which periods?
- Did ownership change alongside changes in the factory's corporate ownership?

### Hotel and industrial enterprise

- What formal role did the hotel have within the industrial complex?
- Was it used by company representatives, customers, suppliers, engineers or other commercial visitors travelling through Central Europe?
- Do company correspondence, advertisements, trade directories, guest records or account books document this role?
- Was the hotel used for meetings, sales activity, exhibitions or negotiations connected with the factory?

### Hotel and railway

- What was the relationship between Hotel Borek and the railway stop/station?
- Which railway administrative or passenger functions, if any, operated from hotel premises before the substantial station complex was completed?
- How did passenger and freight services connect the hotel and factory to wider Central European markets?
- What was the formal classification and importance of the local railway stopping point at different dates?

### People and networks

Research should identify documented hotel owners, managers, staff and guests where records survive, and connect them to relevant people, companies and places without inferring relationships solely from proximity.

### Buildings and landscape

The research record should collect:

- historical photographs and postcards;
- cadastral maps and ownership records;
- architectural plans where available;
- aerial photographs;
- railway plans;
- advertisements and directories;
- evidence of alterations, later uses, demolition or survival.

## Relationship to the wider archive

Hotel Borek should be linked to parallel records for:

- Železárna Borek and predecessor/successor industrial enterprises;
- Strousberg-era industry;
- Brandeis–Eisenschimmel;
- Zbirov/Zbiroh railway stop and station;
- the substantial early-twentieth-century station complex;
- Central European commercial networks;
- the First World War and post-1918 transition;
- interwar industry and the Depression;
- occupation and the Second World War;
- postwar nationalisation and industrial reorganisation.

## Sources to locate and catalogue

Priority sources include company and factory inventories/catalogues, commercial correspondence, railway records, hotel advertisements, trade directories, cadastral records, postcards, newspapers, archival photographs and any surviving hotel records.

Every digital source should preserve its original institution/catalogue link, title, date, archival reference and access date where available.

## Current status

**Research in progress.**

The archive has identified Hotel Borek as sufficiently significant to warrant its own research branch. Specific historical propositions will be added as sourced records are recovered and catalogued.
