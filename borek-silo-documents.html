Provenance

These photographs correspond to an Aukro listing titled “Železárna Borek, pošta Kařez u Zbiroha....třicátá léta.....”, listing no. 7034293029, offered by seller Jaro1942. Aukro records the listing as posted on 20 March 2023 and ended on 30 March 2023 without a sale. The description identifies the object as approximately A4, four pages, with damaged edges. A later listing of the same title and description, no. 7037107486, was posted on 30 April 2023 and ended on 30 May 2023. The seller's dating to the 1930s is recorded here as listing information, not independently established dating.

Research questions connected with these documents

The documents may help establish when Železárna Borek marketed or manufactured silo equipment based on the Richard Aurich system, what Czechoslovak patent or licence arrangements existed, and whether this agricultural and storage technology helps explain the later use of the Borek factory site for grain storage.

The page deliberately separates the source images from conclusions about the date, patent ownership, licensing arrangements or later history of the factory.
