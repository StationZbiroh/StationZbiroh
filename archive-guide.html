How to use this archive

A guide to the structure, evidential language and limits of an independent research project about Zbiroh station and the Borek–Kařez historical landscape.

Scope

The archive brings together material concerning the former Zbiroh railway station, the industrial development of Borek, related people and companies, Hotel Borek, property history and the surrounding landscape. It is a research archive in progress, not a completed institutional history.

The project is maintained at the former station by its owner and restorer. That proximity provides access to the building, local landscape and privately held material; it also makes transparency about interpretation and present-day interests especially important.

Evidence-status language

Documented
Directly supported by an identified primary record or physical object. The source should be named and, where possible, linked or catalogued.
Strong secondary evidence
Supported by a reputable later work, but the underlying primary record has not yet been examined by this project.
Working interpretation
An explanation drawn from evidence, clearly separated from the evidence itself and open to revision.
Hypothesis
A proposition being tested. It must not be repeated as an established conclusion.
Research lead
A name, date, publication or connection worth investigating but not yet sufficiently verified.
Unresolved
Evidence is absent, incomplete or contradictory. The archive records the gap instead of silently resolving it.

Reading the sources

A historical object can establish only what it actually shows. A postcard may document the appearance of a building and provide dating evidence, but it does not automatically establish who owned the building or why it was constructed. A memoir is primary evidence of its author's testimony and self-presentation, but not independent proof of every event described. A later history may provide an excellent route to primary material while still requiring verification.

Core rule: the strength of the language must not exceed the strength of the evidence.

Evidence → context → interpretation → conclusion.

Citation and reuse

When citing this archive, identify the page title, the archive name, the page URL and the date accessed. When the page reproduces or discusses an external historical source, cite the original source as well. Images and documents may have separate rights or restrictions; their presence in this research archive does not by itself establish permission for unrestricted reuse.

Suggested form: “Page title,” Stanice Zbiroh Historical Research Archive, URL (accessed day month year).

Corrections and new evidence

The archive welcomes corrections, contrary evidence, archival references, photographs and local memories. Contributions are evaluated against the same evidential standard as existing material. A contribution may lead to revision, an added qualification, or the preservation of two conflicting accounts until better evidence is found.
